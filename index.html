01 / A little introduction

The person
behind the initials.

Not a résumé or a list of achievements. Just a closer look at the person behind KV.

I'm drawn to the things I don't understand yet. I like asking questions, exploring possibilities, and finding my own way through what I learn.

I'm someone with a curious mind and a lot of interests. Technology is one part of that, but I'm equally interested in ideas, design, creativity and the way people see the world.

I don't want to define myself by one skill or one label. I'm still learning, changing my mind, discovering new interests and working out what matters to me.

This little corner of the internet is a reflection of that process — honest, unfinished and always evolving.
